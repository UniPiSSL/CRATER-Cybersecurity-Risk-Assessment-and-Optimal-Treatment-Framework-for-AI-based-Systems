CRATER Game & Attack Simulator

This is the zero-sum game from the CRATER case study (Section IV). The defender chooses a set of controls within the budget, and the attacker chooses an attack path. Each edge succeeds with probability p × (1 − e) if a control with efficacy e protects it. The attacker's payoff is the product of the edge probabilities along the path (Eq. 6). Solve finds the equilibrium (Eq. 8) and the payoff matrix (Table 6), and Simulate runs the Monte Carlo experiment of Table 7. With a whole-number seed, the results are identical to crater_simulation.py.

Settings

Attack paths

Each path is a chain of edges with a transition probability p. Edge names must be unique.

Controls (Table 5)

Each control protects one edge and has a cost and an efficacy e. The residual is S = 1 − e.
